Interlocking Hearts Necklace: Common Questions

What does an interlocking hearts necklace mean?

Two separate hearts joined into one. It stands for two people who chose to be together and stay connected. It is the classic romantic symbol, which is why it is so easy to give.

Is an interlocking hearts necklace a good anniversary gift?

Yes. It reads clearly as a marriage or relationship symbol at any stage. For ideas by year of marriage, see our guide to the anniversary necklace for your wife.

Is it a good first gift for a new relationship?

It is one of the better choices for that. The hearts symbol is universally understood and does not carry the weight of a commitment symbol like the love knot.

What comes with the necklace?

The necklace arrives on a printed message card in a luxury gift box, ready to give. The card carries your own message, or the sample message if you do not write one.

Can I write my own message on the card?

Yes. After you click “Create Your Gift,” you can type the message you want printed. If you leave it blank, we use the sample message shown in the photos.

Is it real gold or real diamond?

No. The hearts are polished surgical steel with a rose gold finish, not solid gold, and the crystals are cubic zirconia rather than diamonds. That keeps the necklace affordable and sparkly for everyday wear.

How big are the hearts, and how long is the chain?

The pendant is about 0.6″ (1.5 cm) high and 1.1″ (2.8 cm) wide. The chain adjusts from 18″ to 22″ (about 46–56 cm) and closes with a lobster clasp.

How do I take care of it?

Take it off when bathing, keep it away from perfume and abrasive cleaners, and wipe it with a soft cloth. Store it in a jewelry box when you are not wearing it.
