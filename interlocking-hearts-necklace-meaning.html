Double Heart & Interlocking Hearts Necklace Meaning

What two joined hearts really represent — and why it's one of the most meaningful necklace symbols you can give.

Home › Guides › Interlocking Hearts Necklace Meaning

If you've been searching for interlocking hearts necklace meaning, the short answer is this: two hearts physically joined together represent an unbreakable bond between two people. Two intertwined hearts say that two lives are connected and that neither one stands alone, which is why the design is so widely understood and so often given as a romantic gift.

You'll also see this same design called a double heart necklace, an intertwined hearts necklace, or a linked hearts necklace. The names differ, but they describe the same idea: two hearts, connected, and both carry the same emotional weight.

Because a necklace is worn close to the chest, giving someone an interlocking hearts design is a quiet but clear way of saying: my heart is tied to yours.

What Does an Interlocking Hearts Necklace Symbolize?

Best for: Anniversaries, birthdays, and meaningful "just because" gifts

At its core, the interlocking hearts necklace meaning comes down to three ideas:

  • Unbreakable connection → Because the two hearts are physically linked, they can't be separated without breaking the shape itself, which is exactly the point
  • Two people, one bond → Each heart stands for one person. They stay separate shapes, yet they are joined and move together
  • Endless devotion → Much like an infinity symbol, the continuous loop of two joined hearts suggests a love that keeps circling back rather than coming to an end

This is different from wearing a single heart pendant, which represents love and affection in a general sense. Interlocking hearts go a step further by representing a specific relationship: two individuals, two hearts, one shared bond.

Designs that use two different finishes add a further layer. In our Interlocking Hearts Necklace, one heart is rose-gold tone and the other silver tone, a way of showing two different people who fit together. It is also one of the easiest necklace symbols to gift, because almost everyone understands what two joined hearts mean the moment they see them.

Double Heart, Intertwined or Linked Hearts — Is There a Difference?

Not really, but there's a small nuance worth knowing before you buy. In casual search and everyday conversation, double heart necklace meaning, two hearts intertwined meaning, and interlocking hearts necklace meaning are treated as the same thing, and in most cases they are. "Intertwined," "linked," "connected," and "interlocked" are just different ways to describe two hearts joined together.

Where it gets slightly more specific is in the design itself. When the two hearts are truly interlocking — physically hooked through one another — the meaning leans hardest toward "unbreakable" and "joined as one." When the two hearts sit side by side instead of linked, the meaning shifts slightly toward two people who remain close while still being their own person, which is why that version is also popular as a mother-daughter or best-friend gift.

For a romantic gift, the interlocking version is almost always the stronger choice, since it points directly at partnership rather than general closeness.

What Does a 3 Heart Necklace Mean?

A 3 heart necklace has no single fixed meaning, but it follows the same logic as the double heart: one heart for each person. Three hearts are usually read as three people bound together, such as a mother and her two daughters, three sisters, three close friends, or a small family. Some people also choose three hearts simply because they like the look.

If you're buying for a partner, two hearts is the clearer message, since it points at a couple rather than a group.

How It Compares to Other Necklace Symbols

If you're still deciding between necklace symbols, here's how interlocking hearts stacks up against the most popular choices:

What does an interlocking hearts necklace symbolize?

A double heart necklace carries the same core meaning as an interlocking hearts necklace: unity, romantic love, and a deep emotional bond between two people. The two names are used interchangeably in most jewelry stores.

Two hearts on a necklace generally represent two people joined by love. If the hearts are interlocking, the meaning leans toward unity and unbreakable connection. If they sit side by side, it often represents two individuals who remain close while staying their own person.

What does two hearts intertwined mean?

Two intertwined hearts mean two people whose lives are joined together. The hearts stay distinct but cannot be pulled apart, which is why the symbol is read as love, partnership, and a lasting connection.
