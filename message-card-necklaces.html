Message Card Necklace FAQ

What is a message card necklace?

A necklace gift-boxed on a printed card of words written for one person. The card says it in words and the pendant says it in symbol. Read the full meaning.

Can I personalise the message on the card?

Yes. Type it after clicking Create Your Gift and it is printed on the card. If you leave it blank, the sample message from the product photo is printed.

Is shipping free and how long does it take?

Shipping is free worldwide. US orders arrive in about 6 to 12 business days and international orders in 6 to 15.

Are the necklaces real gold or diamond?

No. They are stainless or surgical steel with a gold-tone finish, set with lab-created cubic zirconia.
