FAQ: Necklace Gifts for Your Girlfriend

Is a necklace a good gift for a girlfriend?

Yes. A necklace is worn every day and close to the heart, so it works as a lasting reminder of you. It is a strong choice for birthdays, anniversaries, Valentine's Day and relationship milestones, as long as she wears necklaces and the symbol fits where you are as a couple.

What necklace should I buy for my girlfriend?

Choose the symbol that matches your relationship. Interlocking hearts say you belong together, a love knot says you are committed, an infinity necklace says your love has no end, and a name necklace makes the gift personal to her. If you are unsure, a heart necklace is the safest and most widely understood choice.

What does a necklace symbolize in a relationship?

A necklace usually symbolizes love, commitment and emotional closeness, because it is worn near the heart. The specific symbol adds the message: hearts for affection, a love knot for an unbreakable bond, infinity for endless love.

What is a good necklace for a girlfriend's birthday?

For a birthday, a heart necklace or an interlocking hearts design is a classic romantic choice. A name or birthstone necklace works well if you want something personal to her rather than a general symbol of love.

Are personalized necklaces good gifts?

Yes. Personalized necklaces such as name necklaces show you put thought into the gift. They are less ideal if she prefers a symbol with a shared meaning, or if you are early in the relationship and a name feels like a big step.

What necklace is best for a long-distance girlfriend?

An infinity necklace is a natural fit for long distance, because it stands for a connection that has no end and stays intact across any distance. A message card that says what you miss and what you are looking forward to makes it more personal.

How early should I order a necklace gift?

Order at least three to four weeks before the date. HerAlways necklaces ship worldwide in 6 to 15 business days, so ordering early keeps the gift from arriving late for a birthday or anniversary.
