Shipping Policy

Thank you for choosing HerAlways. Every piece of jewelry we sell is made especially for you and the woman you love.

Processing Time

Every piece is made to order. Orders are processed and made within 2–5 business days after payment, before they ship.

Shipping & Delivery Times

Shipping is free. Total time from order to delivery, including making your piece, is typically:

  • United States: 6–12 business days
  • Canada, UK, Australia and other countries: 6–15 business days

Business days do not include weekends. Your order may arrive in more than one package if you order several items.

Delays from carriers, customs, or busy seasons can occasionally make delivery take longer. If your order has not arrived after 18 business days, please contact us at heralwaysofficial@gmail.com and we will look into it.

International orders may be subject to customs duties or taxes, which are the customer's responsibility.

Tracking

You will receive a tracking number by email once your order ships. It may show "Label Created" for a few days before the package moves, which is normal. For international orders, tracking may not update until the package clears customs in your country.

If you have any questions, contact us at heralwaysofficial@gmail.com.
