About the Jewelry

What comes in the box?

Each necklace arrives with its message card in a luxury gift box, ready to give. Some designs include a box with an LED spotlight; check the product page for your design.

Is it real gold or a real diamond?

No. Where a necklace has a stone, it is cubic zirconia, a lab-created stone, not a diamond. The metal is plated or finished (for example a white gold or rose gold finish over steel), not solid gold. This keeps the pieces affordable while giving a bright, sparkling look. Exact materials are listed on each product page.

How do I take care of it?

Take it off when bathing, keep it away from perfume and abrasive cleaners, and wipe it with a soft cloth. Store it in its box when you are not wearing it.

Shipping & Delivery

Is shipping really free?

Yes. Shipping is free on all orders, including international orders.

How long does shipping take?

Orders are made to order and processed in 2–5 business days. Total delivery time is about 6–12 business days in the US and 6–15 business days for other countries. Order early if you are buying for a specific date. See our shipping policy for details.

Will I get tracking information?

Yes, you’ll receive a tracking number by email once your order ships. It may show “Label Created” for a few days before the package moves, which is normal. For international orders, tracking may not update until the package clears customs.

What if my order hasn’t arrived?

Delays from carriers, customs or busy seasons can sometimes happen. If your order has not arrived after 18 business days, contact us and we will look into it.

Will I have to pay customs or import duties?

International orders may be subject to customs duties or taxes, which are the customer’s responsibility.
