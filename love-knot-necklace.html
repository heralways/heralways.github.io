Love Knot Necklace: Common Questions

What does a love knot necklace mean?

The love knot has no clear beginning or end, so it represents a bond that cannot be untangled: two lives tied together. Read the full story in our love knot necklace meaning guide.

Is a love knot necklace a good first gift?

Usually not. It is traditionally chosen once a relationship has moved past the early stages into something you both know is lasting. For an earlier gift, the Interlocking Hearts or Infinity necklace is a lighter choice.

Is it a good anniversary gift?

Yes, it is one of the best fits for an anniversary that marks a real milestone. See our guide to the anniversary necklace for your wife for what to give by year.

What comes with the necklace?

Is it real gold or a real diamond?

No. The necklace is stainless steel with a 14k white gold finish, not solid gold, and the stone is cubic zirconia rather than a diamond. That keeps it more affordable while giving a bright, sparkling look.

How big is the pendant, and how long is the chain?

The pendant is a small, delicate piece, about 0.6″ (15.7 mm) high and 0.23″ (6 mm) wide, even though the symbol itself is a serious one. The chain adjusts from 18″ to 22″ (about 46–56 cm) and closes with a lobster clasp.

How do I take care of it?

Take it off when bathing, keep it away from perfume and abrasive cleaners, and wipe it with a soft cloth. Store it in a jewelry box when you are not wearing it.
